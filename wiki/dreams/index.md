# Dream Journal
**Category:** Dreams
**Last updated:** 2026-04-06

## Overview

Jack has been logging dreams in his Notion writing database since at least early 2026. Dreams are tagged "Log" + "Personal" and receive Michelin star ratings like his other writings. Several have achieved Bib Gourmand - the same tier as some of his best essays.

The dream journal is titled with the date and a descriptive subtitle. Jack writes them as observation notes: what happened, the sequence of events, sensory details. He does not typically add interpretation in the Notion entry itself - that is left for later reflection or external analysis.

## Pattern

Both rated dreams share key features:
- **Multiple settings** - dreams shift locations rapidly
- **Technology + place hybrid** - real-world locations blend with Jack's current work context (coding sessions, [[history/georgia-tech-era]], California)
- **Alone at the end** - both dreams end with Jack isolated or waking alone
- **Transition imagery** - portals, walking beyond maps, graduation ceremonies

The dreams are consistent with the writing archive's core themes: ambition, isolation, geography as identity, and the blurring of work and self.

## Why Jack Rates Dreams

The fact that Jack applies his Michelin star system to dreams is itself telling. He treats his inner life with the same systematic rigor as his reading, his running, and his professional work. A Bib Gourmand dream is a dream that taught him something or stayed with him - not just an interesting night.

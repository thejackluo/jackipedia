## Reading

**Roguelike as metaphor:** Roguelike games (Slay the Spire, Hades, Balatro) are built on a specific loop: choose your tools, go deeper, face increasing difficulty, die, and start over with slightly better understanding. The dungeon is a compression of life's escalating challenges.

**The card decks:** In roguelike deckbuilders, your deck is your entire strategic identity. A deck with missing cards is a deck with gaps — incomplete, weakened, missing key moves. The challenge Jack faces in the dream is not fighting monsters but *diagnosing which decks are most incomplete*.

This is an unusually precise dream metaphor. Jack is not being tested on strength but on diagnostics: identify the gaps, assess the damage, figure out what's missing. It maps directly onto his startup work (finding product-market fit gaps), his customer discovery process (finding the right ICP), and his self-analysis practice (identifying failure modes and blind spots).

**Deeper and darker:** The dungeon goes in one direction — down and dark. Jack follows it. The dream does not show fear of the dark, just an observation of the quality of the descending experience.

## Context: March 2025

The roguelike metaphor may also connect to Jack's reading of *Grokking Deep Reinforcement Learning* — DRL is structurally similar to roguelike learning: agents trained through repeated failures in escalating environments, building policy improvements from deaths and near-misses.

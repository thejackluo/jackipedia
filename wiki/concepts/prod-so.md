## Overview

prod.so is a community and platform for product builders — people working at the intersection of product thinking, design, and technology. It operates as a curated network where product managers, founders, designers, and engineers share work, discuss product philosophy, and connect with peers who take building seriously.

The ".so" TLD signals the intended vibe: not a legacy corporate domain, not a VC-backed startup with a .com. A community built by practitioners, for practitioners.

## What It Is

prod.so occupies a specific niche in the builder ecosystem — more product-focused than a pure engineering community, more builders-oriented than a design community, more intellectually serious than a general startup network. The best analogy is a high-signal Slack crossed with a portfolio platform, where the work speaks and the community self-selects.

The platform is used by people who care about how products are actually built — the decisions, the tradeoffs, the shipped reality versus the intended design.

## Why It Matters

In a world where most communities are either too broad (Twitter/X) or too narrow (a single company's Slack), prod.so fills a specific gap: serious product people building in public and in community, with enough shared context that the conversations are dense and useful.

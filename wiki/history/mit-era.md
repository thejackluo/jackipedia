Jack's **MIT era** covers his engagement with Cambridge, Massachusetts — centered on [[projects/agent-school]] and the surrounding intellectual environment. MIT is not Jack's undergraduate institution, but it is a place he has worked from and built community around.

## Agent School at MIT

Agent School operated out of room E38-379 on the MIT campus — a small office space that served as the physical home for early-stage work on AI agent infrastructure. The door sign, hand-written: "AGENT SCHOOL."

![Agent School room with Jack working at desk](/assets/photos/agent-school-room-jack.jpg)

The Instagram story from this period: "If you are in agent school get grinding :)" — a signal of what the space represented: intensity, focus, and the particular energy of working on something ambitious from a serious institution.

## MIT Campus

MIT's campus runs along the Charles River in Cambridge. The architecture ranges from Brutalist concrete to Frank Gehry's Stata Center (the CS and AI labs building, notable for its deliberately off-kilter deconstructivist design). The [[concepts/mit-media-lab]] is in the Media Lab building, Building E15.

The MIT rabbit — a wild rabbit that lives near the campus — has become an unofficial mascot for late-night builders.

![The MIT rabbit, photographed near campus](/assets/photos/mit-rabbit.jpg)

## Cambridge and Boston

The Cambridge/Boston metro is one of the densest concentrations of intellectual talent in the world: MIT, Harvard, Northeastern, BU, Tufts, the Broad Institute, the Whitehead Institute, MGH, all within a few miles. The startup ecosystem that radiates from these institutions is different from Silicon Valley — more biotech, more defense tech, more academic spinouts, more East Coast formality.

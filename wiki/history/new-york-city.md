## Overview

New York City appears throughout Jack's archive as a node: the WeWork nomadic period, the WeWork nomadic period, the meeting with [[people/eddie-lou]] in December 2025, the New York Hacker House 2025. It is one of the cities in active rotation alongside Boston, San Francisco, and Atlanta ([[history/georgia-tech-era]]).

## The City

New York is the densest major city in the United States and one of the densest in the world. Manhattan south of 96th Street has a population density higher than most cities in Asia. This density produces a specific kind of energy: everything is close, everyone is moving, the ambient stimulation level is constant.

For someone who optimizes for high-signal environments and proximity to exceptional people - Jack - New York offers a different portfolio than San Francisco:

- **Finance**: the largest concentration of financial capital in the world
- **Media**: publishing, advertising, journalism, entertainment
- **Fashion and design**: the most aesthetically dense industry cluster in the US
- **Tech**: a growing scene, particularly in fintech, media tech, and AI applications
- **Founding**: a different founder culture from Silicon Valley - more execution-focused, less pitch-focused, more skeptical of hype

## Q House 2023

The first documented New York chapter: [[history/qhouse-2023]], the hacker house Jack co-organized in 2023. This was the crucible of the [[people/kevin-zhang]] connection and the first time Jack operated in New York in a builder-density mode.

## WeWork

During the nomadic period ([[history/wework-nomadic-period]]), Jack lived in a WeWork for a month. Whether this WeWork was in New York, Boston, or another city is not confirmed - but New York is the most likely location given the Q House connection.

## New York Hacker House 2025

Listed on jack-luo.com as one of Jack's organizational achievements: "New York Hacker House 2025." This is a separate event from Q House 2023 - Jack returned to New York two years later to organize another builder-density event.

## Eddie Lou - December 2025

[[people/eddie-lou]] (Harvard psychology, @eddie_the_lou, 143K Instagram) met Jack in New York in December 2025. They spent two weeks building Delight + Weave together. The December 2025 New York meeting is the most recent documented New York chapter.

## New York in Jack's Writing

The "city at night" aesthetic that runs through Jack's photos and writing has a strong New York connection: the rooftop meditation photo (city lights behind him), the neon rain photo (could be NYC or another dense city), the general attraction to vertical density and night-time urban environments.

New York at night - from a rooftop, from a bridge, from a high floor - looks like what the future is supposed to feel like. This is not a coincidence for someone whose aesthetic is consistently described as "cyberpunk" or "cinematic."

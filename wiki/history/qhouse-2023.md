**Q House** was a hacker house Jack co-organized in San Francisco in 2023. It is one of Jack's notable organizational achievements alongside the New York Hacker House (2025) and inspiring the Georgia Tech Hacker House (2025).

## The Hacker House Model

A hacker house is a live-work shared space where builders, engineers, and founders congregate — eating, sleeping, and working in close proximity. The model produces a specific kind of social density: you can't avoid each other. The ambient conversations, shared meals, and late-night sessions compound into relationships and collaborations that wouldn't form in a conventional setting.

The hacker house tradition in tech goes back to early Silicon Valley. The modern version is more intentional — people specifically choose to move in together because they want the density.

## Kevin Zhang Connection

[[people/kevin-zhang]] — Jack's co-founder on [[projects/mira]] (Efficore) — connected through Q House 2023. The Q House experience was the crucible of that working relationship: shared space over time produces trust faster than most other environments.

## San Francisco as a Hacker House City

Most hacker houses cluster in San Francisco and the Bay Area — proximity to VCs, other founders, and talent. The city has a particular energy for this kind of experiment: enough density, enough ambient ambition, and enough cheap commercial space (at least in 2023) to make live-work arrangements viable.

Jack's San Francisco story runs through Q House 2023, then out to Boston and Atlanta before circling back. [[history/san-francisco]] documents the city itself.

## Overview

A late-2025 entry that closes the loop on themes running through the entire archive. The title clusters three forces that appear across years of writing: fear (of mediocrity, of being ordinary), connection (the chronic struggle to find peers), and perfectionism (wanting to be seen as excellent).

## The Triad

These three forces interact in a specific way in Jack's psychology:

**Fear** → drives the search for exceptional environments and people, fuels the startup grind, makes ordinary outcomes feel intolerable

**Perfectionism** → holds output hostage to social perception ("I want everything to be perfect because I want people to think highly of me"), which creates the conditions for escapism when work feels imperfect

**Connection** → is simultaneously the goal (exceptional people, anime protagonist crew) and the casualty (lost friendships, social alienation, startup isolation)

The fear-perfectionism-isolation triangle is a closed loop. Fear of mediocrity drives perfectionism. Perfectionism blocks authentic connection (hard to be real when you're performing). Isolation deepens fear.

## The Numbering System

Tracks use a three-digit numbering system: 007, 011, etc. This suggests a catalog of at least 11 tracks with a structured production archive - more methodical than the typical casual SoundCloud approach.

## The "011 - mom" Track

Jack flags this as his latest and is explicitly excited about it. A track titled "mom" in a numbered experimental catalog is notable - it breaks the abstract titling pattern of "mirrox" and "intersection" for something immediate and personal. The numbered catalog is how you organize production work; naming one "mom" is how you mark something as different.

## Musical Style

"Most pieces are experimental" - this is consistent with the broader Jack aesthetic: not optimizing for accessibility, optimizing for something specific that he finds interesting. The intersection of experimental music production and technical/systematic thinking (he builds AI agents, he thinks about memory architectures, he designs systems) is not a coincidence. Music production and software architecture share a structure: modular components, sequencing, feedback loops, state.

## Connection to Steins;Gate

Jack's Song of Year 2024 is "Hacking to the Gate" from [[interests/steins-gate]]. The Steins;Gate soundtrack (primarily composed by Takeshi Abo) is dense, looping, and emotionally precise - the kind of music that rewards careful listening. This aesthetic maps onto the experimental, non-commercial angle of Jack's own production.

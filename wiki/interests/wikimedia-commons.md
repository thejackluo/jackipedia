## The Pattern

All 23 images are own-work photographs under CC BY-SA 4.0. The upload timeline clusters in two periods: late 2022 (Anime Expo, Michelin dish) and November 2025 (the big sprint — 15+ images in one day). The subjects — fine dining, Georgia Tech life, robotics, food trucks, hacker houses, senior prom — are a compressed autobiography.

The detail in the image descriptions is notably careful. "Characteristic intramuscular marbling typical of high grade wagyu beef." "Staged boxing match during a robotics exhibition." "The T was briefly missing from Truist Plaza." Jack writes captions the way someone writes who cares that the record is accurate.

## The Jensen Huang Keynote

CES 2026 was dominated by Jensen Huang's Nvidia keynote — "The Age of Physical AI" — a 2+ hour address covering robotics, embodied AI, and digital twins. Jack wrote a detailed analysis of it (documented in `wiki/writings/ces-2026-nvidia-keynote.md`).

The keynote announced:
- **Cosmos** — a world foundation model for physical AI and robotics
- **Nvidia Digits** — a personal AI supercomputer
- **Project DIGITS** and robotics simulation platforms
- The broader thesis: the next wave of AI is physical, not just digital

Jack's Wikipedia editing during November–December 2025 (expanding the Humanoid Robot and Nvidia GTC articles) was CES 2026 preparation — building conceptual infrastructure before the show.
